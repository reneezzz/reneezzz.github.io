Ph.D. student · Human–Computer Interaction
Indiana University Bloomington · Bloomington, IN, USA

I am a Ph.D. student at Indiana University Bloomington, working in human-computer interaction (HCI). My research focuses on human–AI relationships, particularly AI companionship and intimacy.

I study how people form and shape relationships with AI agents through customization and everyday interaction. I am particularly interested in power dynamics and relationship configuration: how people shape an AI’s apparent personality and relational role, and how these interactions influence their own sense of agency and connection. My work draws on interviews and participatory design to explore how people experience and navigate relationships with AI agents.
